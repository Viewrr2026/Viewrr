# Viewrr Data Retention Schedule

Reviewed 18 October 2026 · Viewrr Limited

This schedule explains what happens to your data when you confirm account
deletion. The in-app deletion screen shows your current request, any outstanding
obligations and the relevant retention details.

## Requesting deletion

In the app, open Profile → Privacy → Delete account. Confirm with your password.
A request awaiting password confirmation does not authorise deletion. Once
confirmed, the request is saved before account cleanup starts. If cleanup is
interrupted, the saved request can be retried without asking you to confirm again.

Open projects, unsettled invoices, transfers or payouts, an active subscription,
or responsibility for an agency can affect when cleanup completes. Your deletion
screen explains the outstanding items and the scheduled review date. Contact
[support@viewrr.co.uk](mailto:support@viewrr.co.uk) about a delayed request or to
exercise your data rights. A review date is not a promise that every outstanding
financial or contractual item will be resolved on that date.

## What is removed

| Data | Handling after confirmed account cleanup |
|---|---|
| Account name, email, phone, profile image and profile text | Display identifiers are replaced and personal profile fields cleared |
| Password and login access | Password hash removed, sessions revoked and reset/verification tokens invalidated |
| Push registrations and notification preferences | Removed |
| Profile, portfolio and social links | Cleared |
| Posts and comments | Authored content and media links replaced with removed-content placeholders; thread structure can remain |
| Messages you sent | Authored message content replaced with a deleted-message placeholder |
| Reviews you wrote | Removed, with affected profile ratings and counts recalculated |
| Likes, saved profiles, connections and blocks | Removed |
| Personal tasks and calendar data | Removed; billing-related time records are handled separately |
| Notifications and export-request history | Personal notification/preferences and export-request records removed; actor display details on other notifications are cleared |
| Personal profile, portfolio, feed and message uploads | Access is removed, filenames cleared and stored files queued for erasure; processing retries if storage is unavailable |

Uploaded-file erasure covers the original and any Viewrr-generated preview.
Stored-file removal follows the account cleanup rather than happening within
the same response. Files already downloaded by another person are outside
Viewrr's storage and cannot be recalled through account deletion.

## Records that may remain

Financial, contractual and safety records are considered separately from public
profile content. Records may remain where required for an applicable obligation,
a dispute, fraud prevention or another documented retention purpose. Shared
project/retainer deliverables and the other party's work records need a separate
retention review; account deletion does not automatically remove every copy of
shared work.

| Category | Current policy review period |
|---|---|
| Invoices, payments, transfers, refunds, payouts and billing records | Approximately six years, subject to the applicable financial/accounting obligation |
| Shared contractual records, work history and policy acceptances | Reviewed against a six-year policy period and the purpose for which the record is still needed |
| Moderation reports and content flags | Reviewed against a two-year safety policy period |
| Administrative and moderation audit records | Reviewed against a six-year policy period |

These are retention review periods, not promises of automatic removal on a
particular day. A specific legal hold or continuing obligation may affect an
individual record. Contact us for the basis and timing applicable to your data.

Removing account display identifiers does not necessarily make retained records
anonymous. Invoice details, processor identifiers, contract content and messages
written by another person may still identify the parties. Access to retained
records remains subject to the relevant account, project and administrative
controls.

## Providers and backups

Viewrr uses service providers for hosting, database storage, file storage,
transactional email, notifications and payment processing. Their copies and
backups can follow separate retention requirements. Live-account cleanup does
not mean that a backup copy disappears at the same instant. Ask us for details
of applicable backup retention or a particular provider-held record.

## Your rights

You can export your personal data from the app's Privacy screen. To request
correction, restriction, an explanation of retained records or an update on
account deletion, contact [support@viewrr.co.uk](mailto:support@viewrr.co.uk).
You can also contact the [Information Commissioner's Office](https://ico.org.uk/make-a-complaint/).

See the [Privacy Policy](https://www.viewrr.co.uk/#/privacy) for the categories
of information collected and the services involved. This schedule and the
in-app retention information are updated together when handling changes.
