# Viewrr Community Guidelines

**Version 1.0 · Effective 2 September 2026 · Owner: Viewrr Limited (founder/admin)**

Viewrr is a marketplace where clients hire freelancers and agencies for creative
work. These Guidelines set out what you can and cannot post here, what happens
when something is reported, and how quickly you can realistically expect a
response.

They apply to everything you publish on Viewrr: feed posts, comments, profiles,
briefs, proposals, direct messages, agency pages and uploaded media.

They sit alongside the [Terms of Service](https://www.viewrr.co.uk/#/terms) and
the [Privacy Policy](https://www.viewrr.co.uk/#/privacy). Where the Terms cover
your contract with Viewrr, these Guidelines cover your conduct towards other
people on it.

---

## 1. What is not allowed

Content in this section is removed when we find it. In most cases it is blocked
before it publishes, and repeat or severe breaches end in suspension.

### 1.1 Hate and harassment

- Slurs or dehumanising language targeting race, ethnicity, nationality,
  religion, disability, sex, gender identity or sexual orientation.
- Targeted abuse or pile-ons directed at a specific person, on or off Viewrr.
- Threats of violence, wishes of harm, or celebrating harm.

### 1.2 Illegal and dangerous content

- Any sexual content involving minors. This is removed on sight, the account is
  suspended permanently, and it is reported to the appropriate authority. There
  is no appeal and no warning.
- Content that facilitates a crime, incites violence, or coordinates harm.
- Sale of weapons, controlled drugs, stolen goods or stolen credentials.

### 1.3 Privacy and impersonation

- Publishing someone's home address, phone number, private email, financial
  details or documents without their consent (doxxing).
- Impersonating another person, brand, agency or Viewrr itself.
- Claiming an accreditation, award, client or credit that is not yours.
  Accreditation badges are issued by Viewrr and cannot be self-awarded.

### 1.4 Adult content

Viewrr is a professional marketplace, not an adult platform. Pornographic or
sexually explicit material is not allowed, including in portfolio work. Nudity
in a clearly artistic or editorial context (fashion, fine art, documentary) is
allowed if it is not sexually explicit and is relevant to the work being shown.

### 1.5 Spam and platform abuse

- Bulk, repetitive or automated posting; engagement farming ("follow for
  follow", "like this and I'll like yours").
- Multi-level marketing, "business opportunity" recruitment, crypto or
  investment solicitation.
- Unrelated advertising, link dumping, or scraping other users' contact details
  to market to them.

### 1.6 Taking work and payment off Viewrr

Do not solicit payment outside Viewrr for work sourced through Viewrr. This is
not a rule about our fee — off-platform payment removes escrow, dispute
resolution, invoices and payout protection for both sides, and every serious
scam on marketplaces like this one starts with "let's just do it over bank
transfer".

Posting contact details (email, phone, WhatsApp, Telegram) in public content so
work can move off-platform is treated as solicitation. Exchanging contact
details **after** a project is live is fine and normal.

### 1.7 Media links

Media attached to a post must be served over `https:` from a host on our
allow-list (Viewrr's own storage, YouTube, Vimeo, Cloudinary, Unsplash, Imgur,
Behance, Dribbble). `javascript:`, `data:`, `file:` and other schemes are
rejected outright, because they are attack vectors rather than media.

If you need a host added, contact us — the list is short on purpose.

## 2. How enforcement works

We use two tiers. Both are run by Viewrr, with no third-party moderation
service and no automated account bans.

### Tier 1 — blocked at publish

Content matching a clearly prohibited rule (section 1.1, 1.2, 1.3, over-length
bodies, and unsafe media URLs) is **rejected before it publishes**. You get an
immediate error citing these Guidelines. Nothing is stored, nothing is shown to
anyone else, and no strike is recorded — you can edit and try again.

Automated matching is deliberately narrow. It catches unambiguous cases only,
because a false positive that blocks legitimate work is a real cost to you.

### Tier 2 — published, then reviewed

Content that is ambiguous — possible off-platform solicitation, contact details
in public, possible spam patterns, possible targeted abuse — **publishes
normally** and is added to a review queue for a human to look at. You are not
notified and nothing happens to your account unless a review finds an actual
breach.

This is the deliberate trade-off: we would rather review a borderline post after
it goes up than block your work on a guess.

### Reports

Any signed-in user can report a post, comment, profile or project. Reports go to
the same queue. Reporting in bad faith (mass-reporting a competitor, for
instance) is itself a breach.

### Outcomes

| Outcome | When |
|---|---|
| No action | Reviewed and found not to breach these Guidelines. |
| Content removed | The specific post/comment is deleted; the author is notified with the reason. |
| Warning | First minor breach. Notified in-app. |
| Suspension | Repeat breaches, or a single serious one. You cannot sign in; existing projects and payments are unaffected and support will work through them with you. |
| Permanent ban | Section 1.2 content, or repeated suspension. |

You can appeal any outcome by replying to the notification or emailing
[support@viewrr.co.uk](mailto:support@viewrr.co.uk). Appeals are read by the
founder.

## 3. Response times — what we can actually commit to

**This section is deliberately more conservative than the Terms of Service.**

The Terms of Service (Complaints section, `client/src/pages/Terms.tsx`) state
that Viewrr aims to "acknowledge all complaints within 2 business days and
resolve them within 14 business days". That wording is about complaints, and no
commitment has ever been published for **moderation reports** — which is the gap
this section fills.

We are not going to extend the 2-day figure to reports. Viewrr is operated by a
solo founder, and a 2-business-day acknowledgement on every report, every week
of the year, is **not achievable** — it is broken by a single week of illness or
annual leave, and a promise that breaks the first time someone takes a holiday is
worse than no promise. The complaints commitment in the Terms remains in force
as written; the table below is what applies to reports.

What we commit to instead:

| Report type | Acknowledged | Resolved |
|---|---|---|
| Child safety, credible threat of violence, or doxxing | As soon as it is seen, and always same day if reported between 09:00 and 21:00 UK time | Same day |
| Harassment, hate speech, impersonation | 3 working days | 10 working days |
| Spam, off-platform solicitation, other tier-2 flags | 5 working days | 20 working days |
| Appeals | 5 working days | 20 working days |

**Working days** means Monday to Friday excluding English public holidays.

Two things follow from being solo-operated, and we would rather state them than
have you discover them:

1. **There is no 24/7 cover.** Overnight and weekend reports are seen the next
   working day, except the top row of that table, which is checked daily.
2. **During an extended absence, the queue waits.** If the founder is away for
   more than five working days, a notice goes up in-app so you are not guessing.
   Urgent safety reports are still monitored.

Where this table and the Terms of Service conflict, **the Terms are the binding
commitment** and this table is the operational detail. The recommendation to
Viewrr Limited is that the Terms' complaints wording be reviewed against the same
solo-operator reality before the next version is published.

## 4. Blocking

You can block any user. A block is symmetric: neither of you sees the other's
posts, comments or profile in discovery, and neither can message, comment on or
notify the other.

**Blocking does not break an active project.** If you and the person you block
share a live project, that project, its stages, its messages and its payments and
invoices keep working normally. A block is a social tool, not a way to walk out
of a contract or withhold delivery. To end a project, use the project's own
cancellation flow or contact support.

You can review and undo your blocks in Settings.

## 5. Your content and your rights

- You keep ownership of what you post. See the Terms of Service for the licence
  you grant Viewrr to display it.
- You can delete your own posts and comments at any time.
- You can request a copy of your data, or deletion of your account, in Settings.
  What happens to each category of data — and what we are legally required to
  keep — is set out in [`RETENTION_SCHEDULE.md`](./RETENTION_SCHEDULE.md).
